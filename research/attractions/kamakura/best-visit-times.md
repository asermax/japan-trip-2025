## Seasonal Overview

Spring and autumn represent ideal visiting windows offering comfortable climate, moderate crowds, and abundant natural beauty. [🔗](https://www.agatetravel.com/japan/kamakura/best-time-to-visit.html)

## Spring (March-May) - Ideal Season

**Climate:** Mild, comfortable (10-20°C / 50-68°F), low-moderate humidity
**Cherry Blossom Period (Late March-Early April):**
- Peak bloom: Late March-early April
- Viewing spots: Genjiyama Park, Engakuji, Tsurugaoka Hachimangu
- Challenge: Extreme crowding, long lines, higher accommodation costs
- **Recommendation:** Visit early season (late March) to avoid peak crowds

**Mid-Spring (Mid-April-May):**
- Post-sakura period: Reduced crowds
- Most comfortable temperature range
- Fewer school groups
- Lush spring foliage
- **Optimal spring window**

## Summer (June-August) - Manageable

**Hydrangea Season (Mid-June-Early July):**
- Peak bloom: Mid-June through early July
- Featured temples: Meigetsuin (Hydrangea Temple), Hasedera
- **Extreme peak crowding:** 1-2 hour waits on weekends
- **Avoidance:** Visit weekdays, arrive at opening (6-7 AM), or early June
[🔗](https://knycxjourneying.com/kamakura-hydrangea/) [🔗](https://matcha-jp.com/en/19545/)

**Mid-to-Late Summer (July-August):**
- Very hot (25-32°C), humid
- Peak tourist crowds
- Beach season: Lifeguarded beaches July 1-August 31
- **Special Events:** Kamakura Fireworks (July 18, 2025), Enoshima Lanterns (July 19-Aug 31)

## Autumn (Sept-Dec) - Excellent Season

**Early-Mid Autumn (Sept-Oct):**
- Comfortable, cooling temperatures (15-25°C)
- Decreasing humidity
- Generally dry and stable
- Excellent for outdoor exploration

**Peak Autumn Foliage (November-Early December):**
- **Optimal timing:** Mid-November to early December
- Featured locations: Temple gardens, Meigetsuin window, Genjiyama Park, hiking trails
- Color transformation: Red, orange, golden hues
- Peak duration: 2-3 weeks

**2025 Special Events:**
- **November 23-December 8:** Hasedera Temple Autumn Illuminations (evening until 7:30 PM)
- **November 16:** Gochinza Kinensai (Tsurugaoka Hachimangu founding anniversary)
- **Early November:** Treasure Display (Kenchoji, Engakuji - normally-hidden treasures)

## Winter (Dec-Feb) - Less Ideal

**Advantages:**
- Minimal crowds (lowest all year)
- Clear days: Crystal visibility
- Photography: Sharp atmospheric conditions
- Mount Fuji views: Clear mountain views

**Disadvantages:**
- Cold weather (5-15°C / 41-59°F)
- Reduced hours at some attractions
- Less comfortable outdoor exploration

## Daily Timing Strategy

**Optimal Arrival:** 9:00-10:00 AM (just after openings)
**Tour Bus Arrival:** 10:00-11:00 AM (beat this window)
**Crowd Peak:** 11:00 AM-4:00 PM
**Evening Dissipation:** After 4:00 PM crowds reduce significantly

## Avoidable Periods

- **Golden Week:** April 29-May 6 (extreme crowding)
- **Obon Period:** Mid-August (summer vacation peak)
- **New Year:** December 28-January 4
- **Hydrangea Peak Weekend:** June 14-15, 21-22

**Recommendation for First-Time Visitors:** **Autumn (mid-September through November)** offers most versatile season with comfortable weather, manageable crowds (except foliage peak), and diverse activities.
