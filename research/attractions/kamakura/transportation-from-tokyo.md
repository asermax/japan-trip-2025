# Transportation from Tokyo Research

**Location:** Tokyo to Kamakura rail connections
**Category:** Transportation & Access
**Cost:** 950 yen (one-way), covered by JR Pass
**Best Time:** Off-peak hours (10 AM-3 PM)
**Duration:** 50-60 minutes
**Research Completed:** 2025-11-12

## Route Options

**JR Yokosuka Line:**
- **Tokyo Station Origin:** 55 minutes, ¥950
- **Shinagawa Station Origin:** 50 minutes, ¥740 (cheaper option)
- **Service:** Frequent direct trains
- **Stops:** Shinagawa, Yokohama, Kita-Kamakura, Kamakura
- **Note:** Some trains bound for Zushi/Yokosuka/Kurihama go direct; trains for Kozu/Odawara require change at Ofuna
[🔗](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3115.html) [🔗](https://matcha-jp.com/en/1938/)

**JR Shonan Shinjuku Line:**
- **Shinjuku Station:** 57 minutes, ¥950
- **Shibuya Station:** 52 minutes, ¥940
- **Ikebukuro Station:** 63 minutes, ¥940
- **Service:** Direct to Kamakura, multiple trains per hour
[🔗](https://www.shinjukustation.com/jr-shonan-shinjuku-line-for-yokohama-kamakura/)

## JR Pass Coverage

Both routes **fully covered by Japan Rail Pass** for foreign tourists, making them optimal for JR Pass holders. [🔗](https://www.jrailpass.com/blog/visit-kamakura-with-the-japan-rail-pass)

## IC Card Option

Suica and Pasmo IC cards function at all Tokyo-area and Kamakura stations for convenient tap-and-ride payment without paper tickets. [🔗](https://www.japan-experience.com/plan-your-trip/travel-by-train/japan-itineraries/how-to-get-from-tokyo-to-kamakura)

## Station Information

**Kamakura Station Exits:**
- **East Exit:** Komachi-dori, Tsurugaoka Hachimangu, central attractions
- **West Exit:** Beaches, Hasedera Temple, western attractions
- **Facilities:** Luggage storage, ATMs, restrooms, ticket offices

## Practical Tips

- **Peak Hours:** 7:30-9:00 AM and 5:00-7:00 PM (maximum crowding)
- **Optimal Timing:** 10:00 AM-3:00 PM (comfortable with empty seats)
- **Weekday vs Weekend:** Weekdays less crowded overall
- **Standing Room:** Peak hours may require standing with luggage
- **Day Trip:** Arrive 9:00-10:00 AM, return 5:00-7:00 PM
- **Last Trains:** Around 9-10 PM for comfortable Tokyo return

**Integration:** Multiple daily trains enable flexible scheduling. Use Tokyo-Kamakura route to begin JR Pass activation. Arrive early, deposit bags at station, full-day exploration.
