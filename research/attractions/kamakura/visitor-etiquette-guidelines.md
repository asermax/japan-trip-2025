## Temple & Shrine Entry

**Gate Entry (Sanmon/Torii):**
- Pause and bow slightly (15-30 degrees) before passing through entrance gates
- Marks transition from everyday world to sacred space
[🔗](https://umamibites.com/useful_info/18096/)

## Hand Purification Ritual (Temizu)

**Step-by-Step:**
1. **Right hand:** Hold ladle, scoop water, wash left hand
2. **Left hand:** Switch ladle, wash right hand
3. **Mouth rinsing:** Pour water into cupped left hand, rinse gently (don't drink from ladle)
4. **Discreet spitting:** Spit away from basin subtly
5. **Ladle cleaning:** Tilt ladle so water runs down handle, return to rest position
[🔗](https://www.kanpai-japan.com/religion-and-spirituality-in-japan/good-practices-visiting-temples-shrines)

**Post-COVID:** Some temples removed ladles; use touchless systems provided

**Hand/Towel:** Bring small towel or handkerchief to dry hands after purification [🔗](https://lovefortraveling.com/japan-shrine-temple-etiquette/)

## Behavior at Sacred Sites

**Quiet Demeanor:**
- Whisper or speak very quietly
- Silence phone notifications; avoid calls
- Walk slowly and deliberately
- Spend time in quiet observation
[🔗](https://aichinow.pref.aichi.jp/en/travelkits/abc_temples/)

## Photography Etiquette

**Check Restrictions:**
- Look for "Photography Prohibited" signs
- Ask temple staff when uncertain
- Avoid photographing active prayer sessions
- Respect residential areas; never photograph residents or homes
- No sacred objects or people worshipping
[🔗](https://www.japantravelpros.com/blog/japan-travel-photography-etiquette)

**Iconic Spots:** At Enokoji crossing, respect traffic, avoid blocking pedestrians, stay off tracks, keep noise minimal [🔗](https://visit.trip-kamakura.com/guidelines/)

## Smoking Regulations

**Designated Areas Only:**
- The Tobacco Kamakura Smoking Area (i-ZA Kamakura 3F, 9 AM-5 PM)
- Kamakura Station area prohibits public smoking
- Penalty: Verbal warning → stop order → 2,000 yen fine
[🔗](https://visit.trip-kamakura.com/services/smoking-areas-and-restaurants-in-kamakura-where-smoking-is-permitted/)

## Trash Management

**Pack Out Everything:**
- Trail etiquette: Remove all trash including organic matter
- Street responsibility: Carry trash until proper disposal
- Temple grounds: Remove all items (candy wrappers, tissue)
- Beach areas: Remove all beach debris
- Limited public bins: Plan to carry trash
[🔗](https://visit.trip-kamakura.com/guidelines/)

## Trail & Hiking Etiquette

**Path Adherence:**
- Stay strictly on marked trails
- Don't pick plants or harvest natural items
- Don't remove rocks or alter landscape
- Maintain quiet during nature walks
- Allow other hikers to pass appropriately
- Check trail status (some closed seasonally)

## Residential Area Respect

**Privacy Awareness:**
- Never photograph residents or private homes
- Keep group noise levels low in neighborhoods
- Don't trespass on private property
- Be quiet during early morning and evening commute times
[🔗](https://unseen-japan.com/kamakura-tourists-vandalism-urination/)

## Dress Considerations

**Temple Appropriateness:**
- Cover shoulders (avoid tank tops/sleeveless)
- Knee-length preferred over very short lengths
- Clean, neat appearance
- Wear easy-to-remove shoes

## Dining Etiquette

- Moderate conversation volume
- Slurping noodles appropriate and expected
- Avoid sticking chopsticks vertically in rice
- Finish most food (waste discouraged)
- Wait for staff to bring check
- Say "itadakimasu" before eating, "gochisousama" after

## Money & Payment

- **No tipping:** Japan doesn't practice tipping (can be offensive)
- Verify card acceptance (many places cash-only)
- Temple donations voluntary (100-500 yen typical)
