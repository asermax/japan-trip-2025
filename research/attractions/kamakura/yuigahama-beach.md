# Yuigahama Beach Research

**Location:** Yuigahama, Kamakura
**Category:** Public Beach & Coastal Recreation
**Cost:** Free admission, equipment rentals available
**Best Time:** Summer (July-August), autumn/winter (sunset viewing, fewer crowds)
**Duration:** 2-4 hours
**Research Completed:** 2025-11-12

## Basic Information

![Yuigahama Beach](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:230128_Yuigahama_Kamakura_Kanagawa_pref_Japan02s3.jpg)
*Caption: Yuigahama Beach stretching along Kamakura's coast with views toward Sagami Bay. Source: Wikimedia Commons*

Yuigahama Beach is a 890-meter wide and 40-meter deep sandy beach forming the heart of Kamakura's 7-kilometer coastline along Sagami Bay. Blue Flag certified for excellent water quality and facilities—among the best in Japan. [🔗](https://visit.trip-kamakura.com/things-to-do/yuigahama-beach/)

**Historical Significance:** Sacred ground to Minamoto clan during Kamakura Shogunate (1185-1333), hosting political ceremonies, military training (horseback archery), and religious rituals. By the 1960s-1970s, transformed into birthplace of Japan's modern beach culture. [🔗](https://kamakuraguide.com/en/hase-yuigahama/yuigahama-beach/)

## Access Information

**From Kamakura Station:** 15-20 minute walk (1.4 km) from east exit
**From Yuigahama Station (Enoden Line):** 5 minute walk (closest station)
**From Hase/Wadazuka Stations:** Both approximately 5 minute walk

## Activities & Features

**Swimming:** Shallow water, gentle waves, ideal for families. Official season July 1 - August 31 (10:00 AM-5:00 PM with lifeguards and AI rip current detection system)

**Water Sports:** Surfing zone (west side), stand-up paddleboarding (SUP), bodyboarding, windsurfing. Multiple rental shops along beachfront. [🔗](https://livejapan.com/en/in-tokyo/in-pref-kanagawa/in-kamakura/spot-lj0002320/)

**Seasonal Beach Huts (Umi-no-ie):** 16-20 beach huts during summer offering grilled seafood, drinks, shade, changing rooms, equipment rentals. Each hut has unique character from California-style bars to traditional Japanese seafood grills. [🔗](http://visit-kamakura-stores.com/cathandbook/glossarytable/glossary25.html)

**Sunset Viewing:** Renowned throughout Shonan region for spectacular sunsets. Western exposure creates dramatic orange and pink skies toward Enoshima island. Autumn/winter offer particularly fine conditions. [🔗](https://skyticket.com/guide/42921)

**Off-Season (Sept-June):** Daytime camping and BBQs permitted, dog walking allowed, surfing without crowds, peaceful beach strolls, relaxed atmosphere. [🔗](https://kamakuraguide.com/en/hase-yuigahama/yuigahama-beach/)

## Facilities

- Public toilets: 2 permanent + 1 temporary (summer)
- Free public showers: 3 locations
- Lifeguard station (summer): ☎ 0467-25-0025
- Accessibility: Boardwalks, multi-purpose toilets, amphibious wheelchairs (summer)

## Practical Tips

**Beach Etiquette (July 1 - August 31):**
- NO BBQs, camping, or fireworks during operating hours (10 AM-5 PM)
- Cover tattoos when on shore (strictly enforced)
- No alcohol outside beach houses during official hours

**Off-Season (Sept-June):**
- Daytime camping and BBQs allowed
- Dogs allowed on beach
- More relaxed atmosphere with fewer restrictions

**Best Times:**
- Summer: Full facilities but very crowded
- Early September: Still warm, thinner crowds
- Autumn/Winter: Quieter beach walks, beautiful sunsets, ideal surfing
- Early morning (6-7 AM): Locals, first surfing wave, tranquil

**Nearby Dining:**
- Daisy's Cafe: Surf-shack interior, sunset views
- Seedless Bar: California-style with alcohol
- Cafe Sakanoshita: Traditional with pancakes

**Important Notes:**
- Beach huts operate ONLY July 1 - August 31
- No lifeguards off-season—exercise caution
- Approximately 1 million visitors each summer
- Blue Flag certified for exceptional quality
