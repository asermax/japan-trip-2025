# Enoden Scenic Railway Research

**Location:** 15 stations connecting Kamakura-Fujisawa
**Category:** Local Train Line & Experience
**Cost:** 310 yen (full journey), 800 yen (day pass)
**Best Time:** Weekday mid-morning, late afternoon
**Duration:** 34 minutes (complete journey)
**Research Completed:** 2025-11-12

## Basic Information

The Enoshima Electric Railway (Enoden) is a beloved local train line operating continuously since 1902 (120+ years). The 15-station, 10-kilometer line has become iconic in Japanese popular culture, featured in "Slam Dunk" anime. The train offers intimate coastal and residential views, running inches from houses, through old tunnels, and past shrine gates. [🔗](https://en.japantravel.com/kanagawa/traveling-the-enoden-line/65792/)

**Journey Details:**
- **Stations:** 15 total
- **Distance:** 10 kilometers
- **Duration:** 34 minutes (Fujisawa-Kamakura complete)
- **Operating:** Since 1902

## Route Characteristics

**Unique Features:**
- Trains pass inches from traditional houses
- Historic tunnels create temporal experience
- Train crossings directly outside shrine/temple gates
- Coastal sections along Sagami Bay
- Connects multiple Shonan beach communities
[🔗](https://japantravelplanning.com/enoshima-electric-railway/)

## Notable Stops

**Koshigoe Station:**
- Scenic views of Enoshima Island and Mount Fuji (conditions permitting)
- Original Slam Dunk anime opening scene location
- Premier photo spot for iconic train and island views
[🔗](https://ikimasho.net/2017/03/12/riding-the-enoden-enoshima-electric-railway/)

**Enoshima Station:** Gateway to Enoshima Island
**Yuigahama Station:** Primary beach access
**Kamakura Station:** Main tourist hub

## Fares & Passes

**Single Fare:** ¥310 (complete Fujisawa-Kamakura)
**Noriorikun One-Day Pass:** ¥800 adults, ¥400 children (unlimited rides + 20+ facility discounts)
**Enoshima-Kamakura Freepass:** ¥1,640 from Shinjuku (round-trip + unlimited Enoden)
[🔗](https://www.enoden.co.jp/en/tourism/ticket/noriorikun/)

**Schedule:** Trains every 15 minutes; check www.enoden.co.jp/en for detailed timetables

## Train Characteristics

- Two-car configuration (intimate, small-scale)
- Vintage aesthetic maintained
- Open windows for sensory engagement
- Mix of commuters and tourists (authentic atmosphere)
[🔗](https://gowithguide.com/blog/enoden-one-of-the-most-loveable-local-trains-in-japan-736/)

## Cultural Significance

**Original Purpose (1902):** Practical commuter transportation
**Contemporary:** Iconic tourism experience maintaining local transportation function
**Pop Culture:** Slam Dunk anime opening scene generated renewed interest globally
[🔗](https://specialdialtravel.com/exploring-kamakura-on-enoden/)

## Practical Tips

- **Standalone Experience:** 1-2 hours for full journey exploration
- **Station Hopping:** Combine with stops at Enoshima, beaches, coastal attractions
- **Photography:** Early morning for optimal lighting
- **Sunset Ride:** Evening offers lighting changes, fewer crowds
- **Least Crowded:** Weekday off-peak (9-11 AM, 2-4 PM)

**Important:**
- NOT covered by JR Pass (not JR-operated)
- IC Cards accepted (Suica, Pasmo)
- Keep luggage minimal (crowded conditions)

**Integration:** Plan 1-2 hours for riding experience combined with station stops. Use day pass strategy for multiple stops. Combine with Kamakura temples and beach exploration.
