# AllTrails Kamakura Network Research

**Location:** Kamakura trails network
**Category:** Hiking Trail System
**Cost:** Free trail access (temple admission fees apply at endpoints)
**Best Time:** Spring (cherry blossoms), autumn (foliage), weekday mornings
**Duration:** Varies by trail (30 minutes to 6+ hours)
**Research Completed:** 2025-11-12

The AllTrails network in Kamakura consists of 63 scenic trails offering diverse hiking experiences: 61 walking trails, 60 hiking trails, and 14 running trails with varying difficulty levels. [🔗](https://www.alltrails.com/japan/kanagawa/kamakura) The trails traverse forested hills, connect historic temples, and provide stunning coastal views.

**Top 3 Trails:**
1. **Kuzuharaoka-Daibutsu Hiking Course** (Most Popular) - 2.1-3.0 miles, moderate, 57 min-1.5 hr, 4.4 stars, connects Jochiji Temple to Great Buddha (Currently closed October 2025 due to typhoon)
2. **Tenen Hiking Course** (Most Challenging) - 3.8-4 km, challenging, 2-3 hours, follows ridges with panoramic views
3. **Gionyama Hiking Course** (Shortest/Easiest) - 0.7 miles, easy, 21-30 minutes, suitable for all ages

## Trail Selection Guide

**Beginners:** Gionyama (0.7 mi, 21 min, easy) or Genjiyama Park sections
**Intermediate:** Kuzuharaoka-Daibutsu (when reopened) - 2-3 miles, manageable
**Advanced:** Tenen Course (4 km, 2-3 hours) or Epic Full-Day Circuit (6-7 hours connecting all trails)

**Families:** Gionyama (short, interesting cave tomb), Genjiyama Park (picnic facilities, restrooms, open spaces)

## Practical Information

**Access via AllTrails:**
- Website: www.alltrails.com/japan/kanagawa/kamakura
- Mobile apps (iOS/Android) with offline map downloads
- Features: Detailed maps, user reviews, GPS navigation, real-time conditions

**Best Seasons:**
- **Spring:** Cherry blossoms, pleasant temperatures (15-20°C)
- **Autumn:** Spectacular fall foliage, best photography conditions, peak hiking season
- **Winter:** Cool, crisp air, minimal crowds, clear visibility
- **Avoid:** Rainy season (mid-June to July) - trails muddy and dangerous

**Safety Essentials:**
- Sturdy hiking shoes mandatory (roots, rocks, natural terrain)
- Avoid hiking during/after rain (extremely slippery)
- Download offline maps before hiking
- Water (1-2 liters), snacks, insect repellent

**Temple Connections:**
- Tenen: Kenchoji (¥500) to Zuisenji (¥200)
- Daibutsu: Jochiji (¥200) to Kotoku-in (¥300)

**Important Notes:**
- Daibutsu Hiking Course closed October 2025 (typhoon damage)
- Check AllTrails for current trail conditions and recent reviews
- Weekdays significantly less crowded than weekends
- Early start (8:00-9:00 AM) recommended
