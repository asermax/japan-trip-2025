# Zaimokuza Beach Research

**Location:** Zaimokuza, Kamakura
**Category:** Beach & Water Sports Hub
**Cost:** Free admission, equipment rentals available
**Best Time:** Spring-autumn (off-season), early morning, weekdays
**Duration:** 2-4 hours
**Research Completed:** 2025-11-12

## Basic Information

Zaimokuza Beach is Kamakura's most tranquil beach alternative, characterized by shallow calm waters and evolved into a premier water sports destination for stand-up paddleboarding (SUP), kayaking, and windsurfing. The beach maintains old-world charm with traditional beach houses and authentic local atmosphere. [🔗](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1589/)

**GPS:** 35.306194°N, 139.548693°E [View on Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=35.306194,139.548693)

## Access & Facilities

**Access:** 15-20 minute walk from Kamakura Station or local bus
**Hours:** 9:00 AM-5:00 PM (beach season)
**Lifeguards:** July 1-August 31, 2025
**Facilities:** Showers, restrooms, changing rooms

**Water Sports:**
- **SUP:** Year-round schools, certified instruction, equipment rentals
- **Kayaking:** JSP Kamakura operators, ideal for beginners
- **Windsurfing:** Far eastern end exclusive to surfers/windsurfers, good breezes

**Character:** More local and residential than Yuigahama, fewer commercial establishments, quieter atmosphere, traditional beach houses

**Best Times:**
- Summer: Peak season with lifeguards, most crowded
- Spring/Autumn: Comfortable temperatures, minimal crowds  
- Winter: Very calm waters, cool temperatures, few visitors

**Integration:** Combine with Zaimokuza temples or multiple beach exploration. Use Enoden train to connect to Enoshima Island for coastal day trip.
